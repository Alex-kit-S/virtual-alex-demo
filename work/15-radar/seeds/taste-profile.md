# Taste Profile - Alex's model of you

**This is a seed, not a profile.** It ships empty on purpose. Every section below is filled by run 1
from your own `soul.md` and `vault/me/`, and then sharpened by every decision you make in
[[projects/radar/decisions]].

**Why it ships empty.** A taste profile handed to you pre-written is somebody else's taste wearing
your name. It would score every item for months before you noticed, and you would not notice,
because a confident wrong filter looks exactly like a working one. An empty section is visible. A
borrowed opinion is not.

**Confidence: COLD.** 0 dated decisions. Until there are 20, every score this profile produces is
labelled low-confidence and the radar does not narrow hard on taste (Run Check 4). Cold is the
correct state on day one; it is not a problem to fix by writing more here.

## How to read this
Favour high fit-to-stack, high leverage on something you already run, and low effort. When in doubt,
ask whether the item moves the thing you said matters most. If it moves neither, it is a curiosity.
Score it low and let it go.
