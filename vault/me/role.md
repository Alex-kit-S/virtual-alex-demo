# Role

Juno coaches first-time managers, mostly at software companies with 20 to 200 people. The work is 1:1
sessions plus a small group programme that runs twice a year. Remote.

The practice is [[business/ashgrove-coaching]], and it does one thing on purpose: the first year of
managing people. Team offsites are out of scope, and she says no to them.

What she is working toward is on [[me/goals]]. How she wants Alex to behave is on [[me/preferences]].
