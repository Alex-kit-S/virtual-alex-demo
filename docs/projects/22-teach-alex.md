# 22 - Teach-Alex

## What it actually does
You say "you got that wrong, here is the rule". Alex works out what kind of correction it is (your
voice, a fact about a person, a behaviour rule, a formatting preference), files it in the right
place, shows you the exact edit, applies it once you confirm, and logs it.

Drop a note in the `inbox` folder, or just say it in a session. A plain folder on disk, so it works
from a phone through any file-sync app, with nothing to configure and nothing to expire.

## Why it exists
This is the verb the whole system compounds on. Nothing else here turns a moment of annoyance into a
rule that holds forever. Every other feature gets better because this one exists.

## What it will never do
Edit `soul.md` or any rules file without asking you first, and show you the exact change before
making it. A system that rewrites who its owner is on a guess has stopped being an assistant.

## Works together with
- **soul.md** - where voice corrections land.
- **`vault/people/`** - where facts about people land.
- **[Self-Review](23-self-review.md)** - reads the corrections log monthly and spots the pattern
  behind a run of them.
