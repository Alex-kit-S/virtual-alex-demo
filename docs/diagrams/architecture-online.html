How Virtual Alex works

Nothing is installed. A cloud session borrows the repository, does the work, saves every file back, and is thrown away.
Starts a session
You
The Claude app or claude.ai/code. You open a session on your repository and talk to Alex. Your own sessions run in Default, where the lane guard stays off.

Where improvements come from
The template
Named in system/template-source.json. Nothing arrives on its own. /update checks its tests passed, shows every change, then waits for your yes.
A fresh Anthropic machine, thrown away when the session ends
Cloud session
Claude reads CLAUDE.md, the commands and the skills from the clone. That is Alex. The hooks that guard it and save its work:
GUARDS
SessionStart
Checks the branch, turns the commit gate on, loads the identity card.
GUARDS
PreToolUse
Runs the lane guard before each tool, in Routine and Armed sessions.
SAVES
PostToolUse
Autosave: every Write or Edit is committed and pushed within seconds.
SAVES
Stop
One last autosave before the session ends.
Wall
The deny list
No send, reply, forward, share or delete. No calendar writes. No edits to settings, commands, hooks or workflows.
Wall
The lane guard
Armed: no web at all. Routine and Armed: no gh, no remote git, no identity writes.
Outside
Gmail and Calendar
Connectors on your Claude account. Alex reads, and drafts replies. Alex never sends.
Outside
The web
WebFetch and WebSearch, behind Anthropic's Trusted network list. Radar reads public feeds here.
